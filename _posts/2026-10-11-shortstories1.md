I boarded a community bus near campus, and a woman probably in her late forties caught my attention. <br>
She looked great. <br>
She looked great. She was dressed entirely in blue, with yellow hand bag, samll red luggage bag, and golden accessories. She looked dazzling in those bright colors. I remember thinking, wow, all three primary colors! She must be having a vibrant life! <br>

All the seats were occupied except for one near to her. I quietly sat down and, whether I wanted to or not, I could see part of her face and the phone she held. <br>
Her choice of vibrant colors had already caught my attention, and I feel a bit guilty about watching her, I found myself looking at her. <br> 

She was adjusting her phone's brightness, moving the slider all teh way to the maximum. Then, she took a deep breath. <br>
From what I could tell, she was trying to place an order, but I could not see what she was ordering. I could not make out any further details, but she seemed to be struggling to complete the order. Every so often, she looked out of the window, perhaps checking whether she was approaching her stop. <br>

She would take off her glasses to look outside, then put them back on to read her phone. I could only imagine how frustrating it might have been to keep switching between the screen and the window while trying to finish the order.<br>

'The next stop is Family Garden' the bus announced. She glanced at her red luggage, peering over the rims of her glasses. She tapped something at her phone. She paused for a moment, closed her eyes briefly, and took a deep breath. <br>

Someone was waiting outside the bus station. They exchanged greetings, as she got off the bus.<br>

I kept wondering what story lay between her bright colors and that final, rushed but hesitant tap.
