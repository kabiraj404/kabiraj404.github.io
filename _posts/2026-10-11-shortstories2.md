I was waiting for the bus and noticed that a boy was trying to put his arm around a girl's shoulder. As he was half way though her shoulder he suddely stopped. I don’t know what was going through his mind that made him stop, but he lowered his hand. 

I was standing just behind them, pretending not to notice. 

Within a minute or so, he reached out again and slowly placed his hand on her shoulder. 
She shivered and smiled back at him. 
He picked something off her shoulder without saying a word, then drew his hand away. He looked at her, he seemed happy. He gently rested his hand on her shoulder again. <br>
She smiled and turned to look at him. 
He looked at her, and their eyes remained locked for a long time.

They held each other’s gaze for so long that I started saying in my head, come on, kiss, kiss!
